The structure

Nine parts, one job each

The order matters. Don't rename, merge or drop a part. If a part is weak, the whole reel leaks viewers at that point.

The next layer

Sentence-level structure

Every line in the script has a purpose. Label each sentence as one of these five. If a line is none of them, cut it.

FUNCTION 1

Information

Tell me something.

FUNCTION 2

Curiosity

Make me want the next line.

FUNCTION 3

Emotion

Make me feel something.

FUNCTION 4

Contrast

Surprise me.

FUNCTION 5

Payoff

Reward me.

And then: rhythm

A great reel script isn't just good writing. It's good spoken rhythm. Short, clear sentences. Strong verbs. Easy to pronounce. Natural pauses. Don't make every line dramatic; strong storytelling comes from the order of information, not from exaggerating each sentence.

Prioritise

  • Short, clear sentences
  • Natural conversational language
  • Strong verbs, easy pronunciation
  • Smooth transitions and natural pauses
  • Hinglish stays Hinglish; keep the creator's voice

Avoid

  • Corporate or academic writing
  • Generic motivation and artificial hype
  • Excess adjectives and forced suspense
  • Unneeded rhetorical questions
  • Repetitive transitions and AI-sounding phrases
